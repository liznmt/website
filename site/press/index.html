bio

short — 60 words

lizzy mcwired is a los angeles dj playing tech house, minimal, and deep tech — a sound she calls adhd house: music that moves the way her brain does. a resident on algorythm radio's "no boxes" segment, she builds sets like her attention works — fast, precise, allergic to filler — and rooms move accordingly.

long — the story

lizzy mcwired plays the sound she calls adhd house: tech house, minimal, and deep tech arranged the way hyperfocus actually feels — locked grooves, quick turns, zero filler.

"i was never incapable. just uninvited." she spent years deciding she wasn't invited to the creative room, then built her own — a home setup, then pirate studios lockouts, then boat decks, club nights, and a residency on algorythm radio, where her recurring "no boxes" segment plays exactly what it promises.

she is also the founder of lizzy's lab, a neuro-inclusive sensory-tools company — because the person who needed the perfect transition built tools for it, and every set is a live demonstration.
